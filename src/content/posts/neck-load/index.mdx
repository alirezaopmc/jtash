Your neck muscles pay for that lever all day. The fix is a ten-second exercise and a taller screen. Drag the laptop up or down, then run the chin tuck.

## Then raise the screen

The chin tuck resets you, but a low screen pulls you straight back into the slouch. Put the top of the screen at eye level: a laptop stand plus an external keyboard is enough.

<small>Load figures come from <i>Hansraj, K. K. (2014), "Assessment of stresses in the cervical spine caused by posture and position of the head", Surgical Technology International 25</i>. They are a model of an average adult, not a measurement of you. This is not medical advice; neck pain that spreads into the arm or hand is a reason to see a clinician.</small>
