# 01 — Brief

## Who

Jay Tash (Alireza Jafartash). Software engineer who also works across product, design,
marketing and sales. MSc student researching large language models; teaching assistant.
Has shipped SaaS products end to end (Fryto, Goldstein) and builds Manova's own products (Waypoint).

## What the site must do

Make the right people want to start a conversation. Nothing else.

- Present a **generalist technical person**: deep in engineering, fluent in the work around it.
- Leave **hooks and clues**, not a CV. The visitor should feel there is more to learn by asking.
- **Never state the type of engagement.** No "available for full-time / contract / consulting",
  no "senior engineer for hire", no rates. The people who reach out want any of: a senior
  software engineer, a product manager, full-time, contract or consultation. Let them ask.

## Audiences

| Visitor | Arrives via | Wants | View |
|---|---|---|---|
| Founder, hiring manager, team lead, PM | Shared link, LinkedIn, referral | Can this person solve our problem? | Hire |
| Peer, student, curious reader | Blog, social, search | Who is this, what do they think about? | Casual |
| Unknown | Typed the domain | Either | Neutral (choice is optional) |

## Voice

- Plain, confident, short. One idea per line.
- First person. No buzzwords ("passionate", "synergy", "rockstar", "10x").
- Claims only with proof the visitor can open (a demo, a repo, a post).
- A little dry humour is allowed in the shell chrome (see [05](05-layout-shell.md)), never in claims.
